Consolidated model evaluation and research results

Executive summary

XGBoost was selected in all five nested outer folds for Cardio70k (mean ROC-AUC 0.8019). Its Cardio70k out-of-fold calibration slope is 1.002 (10-bin ECE 0.002), but unified source-held-out calibration is substantially weaker (Logistic Regression slope 0.438; XGBoost 0.350). This is not evidence of clinical readiness.

Research status

Completed: data audit and quality analysis, baseline and deep-tabular benchmarks, internal and nested validation, source-held-out stress testing, visualization, calibration diagnostics, held-out permutation importance, exploratory subgroup/error analysis, and a leakage-safe HPEM adaptation benchmark.

Remaining: independent external validation, uncertainty intervals and threshold-utility analysis, verification of coded subgroup semantics, exact HPEM reproduction on matched paper data/protocol, or multimodal extension if valid linked data become available. These results are research benchmarks, not evidence of clinical readiness.

Interpretation and limitations

  • Cohorts remain separate because provenance and patient linkage do not support a valid pooled multimodal claim.
  • Unified-cohort results are a source-shift stress test, not stable estimates of deployment performance.
  • ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, threshold metrics, and Brier score address different evaluation questions.
  • These models are research benchmarks, not diagnostic devices. Independent external validation, uncertainty/utility analysis, and clinical review remain necessary.
  • CV, nested, and deep benchmark scripts are distinct evaluations; compare their results with that difference in mind.
